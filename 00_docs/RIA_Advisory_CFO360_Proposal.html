Phase 1 delivery commitment

RIA Advisory operates 17 independent Business Central SaaS tenants — one per subsidiary — across multiple geographies and currencies. Every month, your Group Controller spends 8–10 business days on manual extraction, VLOOKUP consolidation, and email-driven intercompany reconciliation — with no intra-month P&L visibility.

i-finsights connects directly to all 17 BC tenants via OAuth, pulls GL data automatically, and delivers live dashboards and reports — with no manual exports, no Excel, no human step in the data path.

Phase 1 commitment: Collections · Invoicing · Revenue · Unbilled Revenue (UBR) · Accounts Receivable — five revenue and receivables reports plus Azure Entra ID Single Sign-On. Deployed on RIA Advisory's own server infrastructure within 3 weeks of engagement start.

Phase 1 · 3-Week Delivery

Scope & Platform Architecture

Five revenue & AR reports + SSO — on your infrastructure.

Phase 1 — What Gets Built
01

Collections Dashboard

Real-time view of outstanding receivables by client, age bucket, and entity. Month-by-month collection rate trend. Overdue alerts by subsidiary controller.

02

Invoicing Report

Invoice count and value by entity, client, month, and vertical. Drill-through to individual invoice lines posted in Business Central. Export-ready.

03

Revenue Report

Recognised revenue by subsidiary, month, and account category. Year-on-year comparison. Breakdown by department and vertical. Sourced directly from BC GL entries.

04

Unbilled Revenue (UBR)

Work-in-progress and unbilled work by project and entity. Revenue recognised vs invoiced gap. Real-time from BC posted sales and GL.

05

Accounts Receivable (AR) Aging

Full AR aging by entity: 0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days. Customer-level drill-through. Group-consolidated and per-subsidiary views.

06

Azure Entra ID Single Sign-On

All RIA Advisory users log in with their existing Microsoft 365 credentials. No separate password. Role-based access controls per report and per subsidiary.
